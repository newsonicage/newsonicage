Quick tips

  • Splash screen: tap anywhere to start a new lead; swipe up to open the Command Center.
  • Passcode: if prompted, enter the code your office set (default 0958).
  • Demo mode: until the office system is connected, the Command Center shows sample data so you can practice the flow.
  • Repeat customer: use the search bar at the top of the Command Center, tap the customer, and intake reopens with their details prefilled.
  • Photos: add job-site photos from the camera or your library when creating a lead or completing a job.
