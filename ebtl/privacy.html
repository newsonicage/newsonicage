EBTL Field Intake — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

EBTL Field Intake ("the app") is operated by Newsonic Age on behalf of Executive Branch Tree & Landscape (EBTL). It is an internal field-operations tool used by EBTL staff to capture and manage service leads. This policy explains what the app handles and how.

Who this app is for

The app is used by EBTL operators and crews. Members of the public do not create accounts in the app; the information it handles is entered by EBTL staff in the course of their work.

Information the app handles

  • Customer lead details entered by the operator: name, phone number, email address, and property address.
  • Job-site photos and notes captured or entered by the operator.
  • A submission audit log (who submitted what, and when), stored on the device and mirrored to secure cloud storage.

The app does not request or collect device location, does not use advertising or tracking, and does not sell any data.

Permissions

  • Camera / Photos — to attach job-site photos to a lead. Only used when the operator chooses to add a photo.
  • Microphone — may be used when capturing video to document a job site.

How information is used

Solely to create and manage service leads and completed jobs for EBTL. It is not used for marketing by Newsonic Age, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running EBTL's field work.

Third-party services

  • GoHighLevel — EBTL's customer-relationship system of record, where leads and job updates are sent.
  • Google Firebase — secure cloud storage for the submission audit log.
  • Address lookup — typed address text may be sent to an open mapping service to suggest matches while typing. No device location is used.

Data retention and deletion

Lead and job data is retained in EBTL's systems as needed to run the business. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information, contact us at the address below and we will act on the request.

Children's privacy

The app is a business tool and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
