Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Newsonic Age is a systems implementation studio operated by BECON Industries LLC, 4290 Bells Ferry Road, STE 134-2282, Kennesaw, GA 30144. This policy explains what we collect when you contact us through newsonicage.com, by phone, or by text message, and what we do with it.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. There is no account to create and no login.

  • When you submit the form on our site — your name, email address, business name, and phone number, plus the answers you select or write: which problems you are facing, how urgent it is, your budget range, and any description you write of the problem and what you want the business to look like.
  • When you call us — your phone number, as shown by caller ID. If we cannot take the call, we record a voicemail and a written transcript of it.
  • When you text us — your mobile number and the content of your messages.

Mobile information is never shared or sold

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the exceptions described under "Who else touches this information" below exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell any of the information described in this policy, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Text messaging

Text messaging with us is always started by you. We do not send unsolicited messages, and we never message a number that has not first messaged us — there is no signup box, no checkbox, and no purchased or rented list.

  • Message frequency — varies by conversation, and is typically fewer than 5 messages per conversation.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • To stop — reply STOP at any time and you will receive no further messages from us.
  • For help — reply HELP, or call us at (678) 903-1255.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How we use it

To answer you, to understand the problem you are describing, to prepare and deliver work you ask us for, and to keep our own records of who we have spoken to. That is the whole list. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

Who else touches this information

We use a small number of service providers to run the site and the phone number. Each of them handles the information only to provide their service to us:

  • Netlify — hosts the website and receives form submissions.
  • Cloudflare — sits in front of the site to serve it and filter abuse.
  • Twilio — carries calls, voicemail, and text messages for our business number.
  • Google (Gmail) — the mailbox where form notifications arrive.

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to. As stated above, text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party.

Spam prevention

Our form includes a hidden field that real people never fill in. Submissions that fill it are discarded as automated spam.

How long we keep it

Form submissions, call records, voicemails, and message history are kept as long as needed to do the work and keep ordinary business records. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the address below and we will act on the request. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.

Children

This is a business-to-business service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
