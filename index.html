STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Asked before
the first call.

What does an implementation cost?

We don't publish a price per phase — implementations are scoped individually, because what a business needs depends on how it operates today. The Business Assessment comes first and costs nothing; the investment is quoted against the plan it produces.

Why is there always a monthly component?

Because we never build and leave. There is no one-time build — the partnership maintains it, updates it, and keeps improving it, so we are still here a year from now when something needs to change.

Do I have to implement everything?

No. Every business starts somewhere different, and nobody gets the whole system on day one. A later phase is proposed once the previous one has visibly done its job and exposed the next constraint.

What is the BACK-BONE™?

The operating layer underneath everything — customers, jobs, properties, history, calendar, and communication in one place. The website writes to it, the automation runs on it, the Field Console is an interface into it. You rarely open it directly, and nothing works well without it.

What is a Field Console?

The mobile app the owner and crew actually carry — your business in your pocket. One tap files a lead; a long press opens the day. It replaces paper, texts, and random phone calls, and everything it captures writes into the BACK-BONE™.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Field service businesses that run crews and trucks — tree services, landscaping, roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and the rest of the list above. If the shape sounds familiar, the model fits.
