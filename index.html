STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Asked before
the first call.

How much does an implementation cost?

Every implementation has a setup price and a monthly price, both listed at the top of this page and on each phase. The Business Assessment that comes before all of it costs nothing.

Why does everything have a monthly price?

Because we never build and leave. There is no one-time build — the monthly plan maintains it, updates it, and keeps improving it, so we are still here a year from now when something needs to change.

Do I have to buy everything at once?

No. Each phase stands entirely on its own. Most businesses start with the Web Foundation and continue only as long as the returns stay obvious. A later phase is proposed once the previous one has visibly done its job.

What is a Business Backbone?

The operational center of the business — leads, customers, properties, jobs, appointments, and communications in one place. Closer to infrastructure than to software: you rarely open it, and nothing works well without it.

What is a Field Console?

The mobile tool the owner and crew actually carry. One tap files a lead; a long press opens the day — route, active jobs, customer history, emergencies, job completion. It replaces paper, texts, and random phone calls.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Field service businesses that run crews and trucks — tree services, landscaping, roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and the rest of the list above. If the shape sounds familiar, the model fits.
